OUR PURPOSE

Making every darshan easier to discover.

Ganpati Mandal Locator helps devotees discover mandals, understand visit details and connect with the people who make every celebration special.

Find your way

Explore mandal locations on a free OpenStreetMap-powered map, then open walking or driving directions.

Useful details

Find addresses, aarti schedules, arrival information, photos and social links in one place.

Community first

Help neighbours and visitors discover local celebrations by sharing accurate, current information.

Our promise

We aim to make local Ganeshotsav information easy to access on every screen. Mandal information can change, so please verify timings with organisers before visiting. We do not claim affiliation with any mandal unless explicitly stated.
